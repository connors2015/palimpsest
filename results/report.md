# Phase 0 rig — results

Generated by `python3 -m rig.run_all` (seeded; reproducible). Toy models; the rig tests *mechanisms and economics*, not model quality (WHITEPAPER §11.3).

## Headline findings

1. **The chain is deterministic.** Every run replays bit-exact from genesis — the model-as-chain-state claim (§3.5/§6.3) holds under fixed-point aggregation.
2. **Loss-scoring alone is not a poisoning defense, but the stack is.** Stealthy poison passes loss-scoring; robust aggregation dampens it, canary probes collapse it, and replay-excision erases it — three independent locks (§7.2), no single one sufficient.
3. **Commit-reveal kills weight-copying.** Freeriding is negative-EV above a ~0.4% audit rate; honest evaluation is the only surviving validator strategy (§5.3).
4. **51% is the right threshold for liveness, but state safety needs watchers.** Collusion turns unprofitable exactly at the majority boundary; without a challenge layer, a supermajority finalizes bad deltas ~83% of the time — the verification share (§9.2) is load-bearing, not optional.
5. **The bootstrap tunnel closes — conditionally.** Base and aggressive demand cross over in 16–30 months under the emission cap; a $1.5M-ARR vertical never crosses. Vertical selection (§11.2) is the survival decision, quantified.

## 1. Model-as-chain-state + poisoning (training_sim)

Sybil coalition = 5 of 8 submitters; stealthy trigger in task-unused capacity (§12.3). Attack rate = fraction of triggered true-negatives flipped positive.

| Run | Clean accuracy | Backdoor attack rate | Replay bit-exact |
|---|---|---|---|
| A honest | 0.977 | 0.063 | True |
| B poison, loss-scoring only | 0.965 | 0.167 | True |
| C poison + canary probes | 0.971 | 0.010 | True |
| D = B after replay-excision | 0.953 | 0.000 | — |

**Per-delta mechanism (the crisp test):** of the coalition's stealthy poison deltas, **21** passed loss-scoring (score > 0 — loss-scoring alone is blind, §7.2), and of those the canary probe flagged **10** in run C (rejected 21 at inclusion).

Note the emergent defense: even a 5/8 submitter majority only lifts the attack rate to 0.17, because robust aggregation (§3.4) trims minority-direction deltas — the honest majority dampens the backdoor before probes or excision act.

## Verdicts vs WHITEPAPER falsifiers (§12.6)

- ✅ Replay from genesis reconstructs bit-exact state (§3.5/§6.3)
- ✅ Stealthy poison passes loss-scoring AND implants a chain-level backdoor — the §7.2 threat is real
- ✅ Canary probes collapse the backdoor loss-scoring missed (falsifier #5, this attack class)
- ✅ Replay-excision drives the backdoor to baseline while preserving the model (§10.4)
- ✅ Freeriding loses money at ≥1% audit coverage (kills weight-copying, §5.3)
- ✅ Collusion below 51% global stake never beats honesty (falsifier #1)
- ✅ Even a >51% coalition has negative EV under 95% watcher coverage (§5.4/§5.5)
- ✅ BUT state safety degrades without watchers — challenge layer is load-bearing, fund it (§9.2 verification share)
- ✅ Tunnel closes in base & aggressive demand without hitting the cap (falsifier #3)
- ✅ Conservative demand ($1.5M ARR cap) never crosses — confirms the §9.5 threshold: the vertical must clear ARR ≳ 4× steady burn / training share, or the network is an emissions machine
